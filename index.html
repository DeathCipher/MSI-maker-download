Three steps from script to installer

Install MSI Maker once, then build an installer whenever you need one.

  1. Choose your script

    Pick the Python script you want to distribute.

  2. Fill in the details

    Enter the details of your app, such as its name, version and icon.

  3. Build the .msi

    MSI Maker creates the installer file on your computer, ready to share.

What MSI Maker does

Standard .msi output
Your users get the familiar Windows installer they already know how to run and uninstall.
No WiX Toolset needed
Nothing extra to download or configure before you can build.
Remembers your options
Your settings are stored locally and are still there the next time you open the app.
A desktop app
Building happens on your own PC, with a simple window instead of a command line.
